Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TK Manager. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our web application to manage TikTok comments.

2. Information We Collect

When you log in to TK Manager using the TikTok Login Kit, we request access to the following information via the TikTok API:

  • Basic Profile Information: (e.g., username, display name, profile image) to display your account on our dashboard.
  • Comment Data: We request access to read and manage the comments on your TikTok videos.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide the core functionality of TK Manager:

  • To authenticate your secure login.
  • To display your TikTok profile and associated comments within our application interface.
  • To allow you to read, organize, and respond to comments on your videos directly through our app.

4. Data Sharing and Storage

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Your TikTok data is used strictly for the operation of TK Manager. We do not store your raw TikTok password; authentication is handled entirely through TikTok's secure OAuth process.

5. User Control and Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You can revoke TK Manager's access to your TikTok account at any time by navigating to the "Security and Login" settings within your TikTok mobile app or website and removing TK Manager from your authorized applications. If you wish to delete your TK Manager account and associated data, please contact us at contact@shopinzo.com.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@shopinzo.com.
